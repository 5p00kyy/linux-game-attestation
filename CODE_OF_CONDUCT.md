# Code Of Conduct

Project participants must communicate respectfully, review technical claims on
their evidence, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or disclosure
of another person's private information.

Security research must stay within the authorized-testing policy. Claims of
vulnerabilities, bypasses, compatibility, or production readiness must be
supported by reproducible evidence and reported through the appropriate
private channel when disclosure could cause harm.

Maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate these
requirements. Serious conduct concerns should be raised privately with the
maintainers or repository-hosting platform.
